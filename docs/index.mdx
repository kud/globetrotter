**Globetrotter** is a travel atlas that lives in your browser. You mark countries as visited, wishlisted or blocked, keep a small journal for each one, and wander a flat map or a 3D globe that carries live safety advisories, a real aircraft in flight, the ISS, the Sun and the Moon. Your atlas is stored on your device and is never sent anywhere.

- [📝 Your atlas](/projects/globetrotter/docs/your-atlas) — statuses, the travel journal, the sidebar, and how to back up, move or reset your data.
- [📡 Live data](/projects/globetrotter/docs/live-data) — where each live figure comes from, how often it refreshes, and the API routes behind it.
